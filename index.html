I am an atmospheric and physical climate scientist studying the role of tropical convection and clouds in Earth's climate and hydrologic cycle. I am a postdoc at the Program for Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences at Princeton University. Previously, I did my PhD in the Department of Atmospheric and Climate Science at the University of Washington in Seattle.


Update! I will be joining the University of Cincinnati as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Geosciences in January, 2027. Please get in touch if you're interested in climate research at UC.

Updates

June 2026. I spoke during the second Weather & Climate Livestream event, a 50-hour climate science marathon. Thanks to the orgnaizers for putting on an incredible show.

April 2026. I gave talks and met many wonderful people at the University of Chicago, the University of Cincinnati, and Iowa State University. Three cheers for the Midwest!

March 2026. Our AGU Advances paper on tropical hydrologic change was featured as an Editor's Highlight in Eos.

March 2026. I presented at the 5th ICTP Workshop on Convective Organization in São Jose Dos Campos, Brazil. The entire livestream is available here.

Feb 2026. New paper "No 'wet gets wetter' in kilometer-scale mock-Walker circulations", co-authored with Tim Merlis and Stephan Fueglistaler, is out in AGU Advances.

Dec 2025. Another AGU in the books--I talked about patterns of tropical precipitation change in what turned out to be a great convection session.

July 2025. Had a great time at my first Gordon Research Conference on Radiation & Climate. Lots of cool talks on aerosol-cloud interactions and 3D radiative transfer!

May 2025. My paper "Internal Ocean‐Atmosphere Variability in Kilometer‐Scale Radiative‐Convective Equilibrium" is out in JAMES. It was fun working with Vlad Munteanu, Peter Blossey, and Dennis Hartmann to decipher these unique RCE simulations.
